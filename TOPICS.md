# DSA Master Roadmap v1.0

> **Interview-first | Pattern-first | Long-term Mastery**
>
> This roadmap is designed for Software Engineering interview preparation with a clear progression from interview readiness to algorithmic mastery.
>
> **Learning Philosophy**
>
> - Learn **patterns**, not individual problems.
> - Understand **why** a solution works before memorizing it.
> - Build from fundamentals to advanced algorithms.
> - Every topic should end with implementation, practice, revision, and interview questions.

---

# Legend

## Difficulty Levels

| Level | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| 🟢 Beginner | Basic concepts and introductory patterns |
| 🟡 Intermediate | Standard interview topics |
| 🟠 Advanced | Frequently seen in harder interviews |
| 🔴 Pro | Competitive Programming / Specialized algorithms |
