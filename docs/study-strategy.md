# Study Strategy

> **Learn Like an Engineer. Not Like a Student.**

---

# Objective

This repository follows a **Pattern First** learning strategy.

The objective is **not** to complete the maximum number of problems.

The objective is to develop the ability to solve unseen interview problems by recognizing reusable algorithmic patterns.

Success is measured by understanding, recognition, and execution—not problem count.

---

# Session Workflow

Every session follows this order.

## Step 1

Read the pattern notes.

Do not code.

Goal:

Understand

* Why the pattern exists
* When it is useful
* Recognition signals

---

## Step 2

Understand the intuition.

Ask yourself

> Why does this work?

If you cannot explain it in simple words, continue reading.

---

## Step 3

Learn recognition.

Before looking at solutions ask

> How would I know this is the correct pattern?

Recognition is more important than implementation.

---

## Step 4

Study the generic template.

Do not memorize problem-specific code.

Instead memorize

* State
* Variables
* Flow
* Reusable structure

---

## Step 5

Solve Foundation Problems

Objective

Build confidence.

Spend most of the session here.

---

## Step 6

Solve Intermediate Problems

These combine multiple ideas.

Focus on adapting the pattern.

---

## Step 7

Attempt Google-Level Problems

These are intentionally challenging.

Failure is expected.

Focus on reasoning rather than speed.

---

## Step 8

Document Everything

Every session produces

* Notes
* Generic template
* Mistakes
* Insights
* Revision dates

Nothing should remain only in memory.

---

## Step 9

Update Tracker

Update

* Status
* Confidence
* Revision schedule
* Problems solved

---

## Step 10

Commit to GitHub

Every completed session ends with a commit.

Example

```
S055 - Monotonic Stack
```

# Problem Solving Strategy

Never jump directly into coding.

Follow this sequence.

```
Understand Problem

↓

Identify Pattern

↓

Think Brute Force

↓

Improve

↓

Improve Again

↓

Reach Optimal

↓

Verify

↓

Code

↓

Dry Run

↓

Analyze Complexity
```

---

# Learning Rules

## Rule 1

Never memorize solutions.

Understand why they work.

---

## Rule 2

Never copy code.

Write every solution yourself.

---

## Rule 3

Recognition before implementation.

Choosing the correct pattern is harder than writing code.

---

## Rule 4

Do not optimize prematurely.

Always derive the optimal solution.

---

## Rule 5

Explain before coding.

If you cannot explain the algorithm, you do not understand it.

---

## Rule 6

Every mistake becomes documentation.

Mistakes are assets.

# Mistake Strategy

Whenever you struggle

Do NOT delete the mistake.

Document

* Why it happened
* Correct thinking
* Prevention strategy

Mistakes are future revision material.

# Long-Term Vision

At the end of this roadmap you should not merely know hundreds of interview questions.

You should possess a reusable engineering knowledge base that enables you to recognize, derive, implement, optimize, explain, and teach every major DSA pattern expected in Google SDE-2 interviews.

---

# Golden Rules

* Learn one pattern at a time.
* Finish before moving forward.
* Recognition is more important than memorization.
* Templates are more valuable than solutions.
* Documentation compounds knowledge.
* Revision is mandatory.
* Consistency beats intensity.
* Master patterns, not problems.
